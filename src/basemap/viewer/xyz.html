Basemap · Tiles

Plain tile endpoint

https://basemap.oceanstream.io/xyz/{z}/{x}/{y}.webp

z0–14 native. Past the last level your client stretches the tile; set maxNativeZoom in Leaflet, maxzoom in MapLibre, maximumLevel 14 in Cesium.

Global to z8 from GEBCO, land to z10, and European waters to z12 from EMODnet and the high-resolution surveys. Deeper than its source in a given place, it is upsampled — as every deep bathymetric basemap is.

No PMTiles reader needed. The same map is also published as PMTiles archives and a MapLibre style, which go deeper still.

Names

Nothing in the tiles is text, so labels come from a layer stacked on top. Here that is Esri's World Ocean Reference — marine and undersea feature names, from the same GEBCO gazetteer. Its country borders and city names come with it; it is a single-layer raster service, so they cannot be switched off separately.

Depths

The contour lines are drawn into the tiles. The numbers are not — they come from the same contour archive read as vectors, so they stay sharp at any zoom and can be turned off. Thousands from z3, five hundreds from z6, and the shelf and coastal steps from z8.
