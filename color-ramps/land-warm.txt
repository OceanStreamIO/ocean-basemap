# Land ramp: EMODnet's hues, deepened so terrain reads.
#
# The sampled EMODnet ramp (land-emodnet.txt) is faithful to their base layer
# and almost unusable on its own at close range: from sea level to 350 m every
# colour lies between RGB 210 and 233, so the whole of southern Norway came out
# as one pale wash. Viewer-side correction cannot fix it either -- the range
# sits in the top fifth of the scale, where raster-contrast clips to white and
# *reduces* the tonal spread (measured: 54 -> 38 -> 21 as contrast rises).
#
# So the lowlands are taken down into green and the spread is opened up. The
# hues still come from the EMODnet sample; they are darker and a little more
# saturated. Above 2,500 m this is the EMODnet ramp exactly, so high ground --
# bare rock, then snow -- is unchanged.
#
# Elevation is not ground cover, and a green lowland is a lie in a desert. The
# first attempt at this ramp put the Sahara and the low desert around the Nile
# under a distinctly olive wash, so the hue of the bottom stops was pulled off
# green towards khaki, keeping the darkening and the spread. Norway still reads
# as vegetation, the Sahara as sand, and the Nile valley comes out green against
# its khaki plateau because it genuinely is lower. Checked in all three.
#
