# Sea ramp: EMODnet's hue, fading to a pale blue shore.
#
# This is candidate D, adopted 2026-11-10, replacing H. The two are identical
# below 125 m. Across the shelf D lightens towards the waterline, from 186 216
# 243 at 87.5 m to 216 232 245 at the surface (luminance 229, 12%
# saturation). H moved only from 180 to 196 in red over the same span, so a
# wide shelf read as one flat blue with no depth in it; D brings back the
# gradient from the shelf edge to the shore. Chosen in the viewer, on the
# north-west Black Sea shelf and the Sea of Azov.
#
# D was the default once before (2026-10) and was replaced because the widest
# shallow banks -- the Great Bahama Bank, 1-10 m across its whole extent, and
# the Central American shelves -- read as almost white. That is still true of
# them; the lagoon tone (COASTAL_WATER_RGB in pipeline/xyz.py, luminance 196)
# now sits well under the shore, which is what keeps lagoons apart from it.
#
# Every channel stays at or under 245 so the brightest stop times the
# bathymetry's 1.12 shading ceiling stays inside 275, which is the headroom the
# lit slopes need. SHORE_WATER in pipeline/xyz.py and SHORE_BY_PALETTE[''] in
# the viewer are the 0 m stop and move with it.
#
# The candidates stay in color-ramps for comparison and the viewer's chooser
# still offers them; ?water=h renders the previous default.
#
