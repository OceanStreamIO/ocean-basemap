# Sea ramp: the EMODnet sample, with the surface end lifted into water.
#
# The sampled ramp (bathymetry-emodnet.txt) is faithful to the EMODnet base
# layer and reads as neutral grey where the water is shallow: rgb(241,242,244)
# at the surface, a chroma of 3, not reaching recognisable blue until about
# 35 m down. A fjord is mostly shallower than that, and an estuary or a delta
# is all of it -- the Tagus and the mouths of the Danube came out the colour of
# paper while the land beside them was green.
#
# Lifted twice. The first attempt took the surface to a chroma of 27, which
# reads as water in the open sea and still not in an estuary, because an
# estuary never gets deep enough to reach the part of the ramp that had
# changed. This one starts at 42 and rejoins the sample at -87.5 m, so
# everything below that is untouched and everything above it is water.
#
# 42 is also the chroma of the viewer's shore fill (SHORE_WATER in app.js),
# which paints the strip between the shoreline and the first rendered pixel,
# and any water the bathymetry does not reach at all -- the Danube delta has no
# tile in any archive. The two have to agree or the join between them shows.
#
