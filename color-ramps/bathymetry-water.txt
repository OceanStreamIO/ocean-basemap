# Sea ramp: the EMODnet sample, with the surface end lifted into water.
#
# The sampled ramp (bathymetry-emodnet.txt) is faithful to the EMODnet base
# layer and reads as neutral grey where the water is shallow: rgb(241,242,244)
# at the surface, a chroma of 3, not reaching recognisable blue until about
# 35 m down. A fjord is mostly shallower than that, so Drammensfjorden came out
# white while the small lakes beside it looked like water -- the lake ramp
# starts at a chroma of 31.
#
# So the top of the curve is lifted to meet the lakes, and rejoins the sample
# exactly at -87.5 m. Nothing deeper than that changes, and the legend and the
# vector strip along the shore in the viewer carry the same colours.
