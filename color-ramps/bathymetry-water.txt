# Sea ramp: EMODnet's hue, carried to the waterline.
#
# This is candidate D, adopted. The ramp it replaces was EMODnet's own shallows
# sampled from their base layer, which is 2.9% saturated at the surface -- grey
# -- and sat at luminance 238 against land at about 215. Shallow sea and land
# were the same brightness, told apart by hue alone, which is the one cue a
# red-green colour-blind reader does not have.
#
# D keeps the hue and raises saturation through the first hundred metres,
# meeting the sampled ramp again at -87.5 m, so nothing below the shelf changed.
# The candidates it was chosen against are kept as bathymetry-water-{a..f}.txt
# and the viewer can still load them; see docs/sea-palette.md.
#
# SHORE_WATER in pipeline/xyz.py and app.js must equal the 0 m stop, and
# COASTAL_WATER_RGB is taken from it too: a lagoon and the shelf it opens into
