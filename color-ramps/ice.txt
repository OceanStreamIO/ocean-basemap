# Ice ramp: glaciers, ice caps and ice shelves.
#
# The land ramp cannot serve these. It is hypsometric -- colour stands for
# height -- and by 1750 m it has left green for the browns of bare rock, which
# is right for the Alps and the Andes and wrong for Greenland, whose surface is
# ice from the coast to 3,200 m. Pulling that band of the land ramp towards
# white would bleach the Tibetan plateau with it, so ice is masked and coloured
# on its own ramp instead (RenderSpec.ice_ramp).
#
# Ice is not pure white. A flat field of 255s has nowhere left to go when the
# light falls on it, so the dome would clip to a silhouette; and snow in the
# open reads slightly blue, because it is lit by the sky as well as the sun.
# These stops stay a little below white and cool as they climb: the margins,
# where meltwater, dust and bare ice collect, are the warmest and dimmest, and
# the high interior the brightest and bluest.
#
# Format: elevation  R  G  B   (gdaldem color-relief)
0        226 230 234
150      232 236 240
400      238 241 245
900      243 246 249
1600     246 249 252
2400     248 251 254
3200     250 252 255
