# Land ramp matching the EMODnet World Base Layer.
# Curated from color-ramps/sampled/land-emodnet.txt (medians per elevation bin):
#   - the 0-10 m sample is dropped: those pixels sit on the shoreline and blend
#     the sea colour in; 0 m takes the 10-25 m colour instead
#   - up to 1750 m the samples are European and change smoothly, so they are
#     kept as measured
#   - above that they mix ranges EMODnet colours differently (Alpine snow reads
#     white at 2250 m, the Tibetan plateau brown at 3750 m), so the ramp
#     continues by hand: bare rock, then snow
