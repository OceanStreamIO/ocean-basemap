# Lake ramp, in metres relative to the lake's own surface -- negative below it,
# the same elevation convention as the bathymetry ramp, because that is what
# the renderer feeds it (GEBCO's bed minus the lake's surface elevation).
# The viewer's LAKE_STOPS mirror these as positive depths, because the vector
# lakes carry HydroLAKES' mean depth as a positive number.
#
# Lakes need their own ramp: on the sea's ramp a shallow lake lands in the
# near-white shallows and disappears against the land. This starts at a clear
# lake blue and deepens from there, so even a 3 m pond reads as water.
# Format: elevation  R  G  B
#
# The shallow stops were scaled down to 236 at one point, to leave the lake-bed
# hillshade room to brighten without clipping to white. That is no longer
# needed: bed_ceiling in pipeline/lakes.py returns 1.0, so a lit bed can only
# darken and the brightest it can reach is the ramp colour itself. The cap cost
# every shallow lake about eleven luminance levels for nothing -- Kara-Bogaz-Gol
# sat at 202 where it belongs at 213 -- so the stops are back as they were.
#
# Below 50 m the stops are pulled 55% of the way back toward the 50 m colour.
# A lake's depth range is not the ocean's: HydroLAKES spans a metre to 1,642,
# and run across the full ramp that made Kivu at 237 m as dark as deep ocean
# beside Edward at 34 and Victoria at 41 -- 40 luminance levels between lakes a
# reader sees as one group. All three depths are correct; the ramp was the
# problem. The span from 0 to 600 m is now 75 levels rather than 107, so depth
# still orders the lakes and no longer separates them into different kinds of
# water.
#
# On 2026-10-18 the stops below 50 m were pulled back a further two thirds of
# the way toward the 50 m colour, judged in context over Tanganyika, Rukwa and
# Malawi: Tanganyika and Malawi stood 50 luminance levels off Rukwa and read
# as a different kind of water rather than a deeper lake. Below 50 m the ramp
# now spans about 15 levels, so depth still orders the deep lakes and the
# bed relief, drawn over the flat tone, carries what the fill no longer does.
#
# Between 0 and 50 m the stops are eased toward the surface colour, by 35% at
# the top and tapering to nothing at 50 m so the deep half is untouched. The
# shallow end held 42 of the ramp's 88 luminance levels in its first 20 metres,
# which is why Kara-Bogaz-Gol, a 10 m salt lagoon, read as dark as it did.
#
# The surface stop is the coastal-water tone, COASTAL_WATER_RGB in
# pipeline/xyz.py, and the stops down to 50 m are eased onto it. A lake and the
# lagoon beside it are the same kind of thing to a reader -- the Camargue's
# étangs sit next to its salt lagoons, the IJsselmeer behind the Wadden -- and
# at 216 the old surface stop drew the lake 35 luminance levels lighter than the
# lagoon. Below 50 m nothing moves, so the deep lakes keep their order.
