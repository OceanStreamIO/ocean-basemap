# Land ramp: muted, with the elevation carried on luminance and warmth.
#
# Adopted 2026-10-15, replacing land-emodnet.txt. That ramp was EMODnet's
# sage-to-beige, and against EMODnet's own tiles the lowlands came out flat:
# their variation is line work and a flat land tone, and ours had neither. This
# one is 65% of the way from a warm family (M) to a neutral grey family (K),
# judged as a pair against the Camargue, the Venice lagoon, Ria Formosa, the
# Danube delta, the Oslofjord and the IJsselmeer. The lowland is a pale warm
# grey, the hills go darker and warmer to 2,500 m, and from there the ramp
# climbs back to near-white for the high ranges. Elevation reads on luminance
# and on the blue-yellow axis, which is what a red-green reader has.
#
# The two lowest stops are capped at 216: LAND_SHADING's 1.18 ceiling times
# 216 is 255, so a lit lowland slope never clips. Above 4,000 m the stops run
# to 250 and do clip under full light, which on snow is what snow looks like.
#
# The land cover tint (processing/landcover.py) and the hillshade go over this;
# flat ground keeps exactly these colours.
#
