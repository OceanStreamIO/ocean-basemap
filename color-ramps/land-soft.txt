# Land ramp: EMODnet's colours, without their hard step to white.
#
# Adopted 2026-11-09, replacing land-muted.txt. To 1,800 m these are the medians
# of EMODnet's own base-layer tiles over flat ground, measured against the DEM at
# seven sites: their land is a pure elevation ramp, the same height being the same
# colour everywhere within a few levels. Above that EMODnet jumps to white at about
# 2,000 m, which blotches Utah and Colorado, and returns to terracotta at
# 3,500-5,000 m, which bands Tibet. Here the ramp climbs through stone and a tan
# plateau instead, reaching snow only above 5,000 m.
#
# On the blue-yellow axis, the one a red-green reader keeps, this spans about +1
# to +29 under a deuteranopia simulation (Machado 2009), against +4 to +10 for
# land-muted.txt.
#
